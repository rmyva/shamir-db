## Items

1. **Detached workers outside the mutation tracker** (P1 class). The cold-table open that continues after its caller was dropped (`shamir-engine` `tx/commit/core/catalog_access.rs`), the operator-cancel release (`shamir-server` `repo_admin/activity/cancel.rs`) and the tx-registry drop release (`shamir-sync` `tx_abort_release.rs`, which a reaper teardown can start after the final flush) run outside the shutdown tracker. Give cold opens a repository lifecycle owner that shutdown drains, and track the release tails of the cancellation work, so a shutdown cannot flush and close while one of them is still writing.
2. **Export drain and rollback arms.** The export has a separate 60 s wait instead of the shared early cutoff; the fallback `ShutdownOwner` keeps the mutation tracker but not the export service; neither the listener-bind rollback nor the observability-bind rollback joins these workers. Use the early cutoff, keep export ownership in the fallback, give both rollback arms a bounded drain, and keep the original boot error.
3. **The maintenance gate does not stop the storage MemBuffer background flusher or TTL maintenance during a freeze** (`shamir-storage` `storage_membuffer/core.rs`): enrol and drain it, or document the narrower snapshot guarantee in the code and the guide (the guide sentence is already narrowed: say which you chose and why).
4. **`TaskTracker::close` is not an admission fence.** Register workers atomically with a shutdown latch. The stateless worker also loses its request-tracking ownership on disconnect (the waiter owns `InFlightRequest` and the session slot): give the worker the activity guard and slot and make the disconnect cancellation explicit.
5. **`persist_table_lifecycle` runs on the reply side, after the owned worker.** A `DropTable` that succeeds after the client disconnected leaves the boot-replay registry entry, so a restart can recreate the table: do the registry update inside the owned, maintenance-admitted operation.
6. **The one-job slot is claimed after authorization and warm-up** (a bounded preparation slot is needed), and the export job is not visible in the activity view: project it using the existing scope and privacy rules.
7. **Test strength.** The shutdown test installs its own tracker, never starts an export and uses two 2 s observation windows plus a process-global probe: use an instance-scoped probe that acknowledges entry into the join, drive a production-enrolled request and a real export, and make a worker parked past its stage budget produce the specific report error and no flush. The denial test cannot tell never-froze from froze-and-thawed: instance-scoped transition counters (zero rises per refusal, one rise and one thaw per admitted exit). Add a builder-driven test for the `when`/`after` refusal on the export form.
8. **Visibility of moved items** is not always the old reach (`DurableStores` `pub(super)` now spans `server_launcher`, `RunGuard` fields at backup-module scope) and the moved registry shortened one post-run callback field comment: restore the old reach where nothing needs the wider one and restore the comment.

## Goal and why
The design rests on claims nobody has executed: that an adapter plus a strict identifier wrapper can carry the hard cases, that derived buffers can be removed by explicit streaming forms, that a root grammar and operand nodes work, and that the cost is acceptable. A wrong claim found after WIRE-DICT-2 costs a protocol version. Decide correctness FIRST, cost second.

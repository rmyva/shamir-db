DECIDED: remove `default` from EVERY variant-level attribute list of the snapshot (do a sweep of the whole file, not only this list, and report the count), and make both the scanner and the snapshot checker REJECT a variant-level `default` as malformed. Actual FIELD defaults stay.
2. **Name normalization uses one casing routine for fields and for variants.** Serde converts a field `foo_bar` under `camelCase` to `fooBar` and a variant by a different rule table (`serde_derive-1.0.229/src/internals/case.rs:56-108`); the scanner leaves fields unchanged (`Source:81-85,215-217,243-305,373-436,509-534`). DECIDED: a field, variant or type under `cfg`/`cfg_attr` that carries or could carry a serde attribute FAILS the scan with its path, unless the snapshot pins the predicate text on that member; there is no silent inclusion and no silent drop.
